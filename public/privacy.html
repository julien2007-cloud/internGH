InternGH

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details you give us when you sign in: your University of Ghana student email, name, student ID, major and level.
  • Your phone number, only if you turn on WhatsApp alerts.
  • Your activity in the app, such as saved listings and reminder settings. These are kept in your own browser.
  • Listings submitted by publishers, including the organisation's contact email (not shown to students).

How we use it

  • To show you opportunities that match your major and level.
  • To send you WhatsApp messages you asked for: a welcome message, alerts when a matching opportunity is published, reminders before deadlines, and listings you send to yourself.
  • To draft cover letters when you ask the assistant to.

We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

WhatsApp messages

WhatsApp messages are sent through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta. To deliver them, your phone number and the message content are processed by Meta under WhatsApp's business terms. Reply STOP in the InternGH chat at any time to stop alerts, and START to turn them back on.

Where it's stored

Your alert preferences (phone number, majors and level) and published listings are stored in a secured database hosted by our providers (Vercel and Upstash). Everything else stays in your browser. Access keys are kept on our servers and never sent to your device.

How long we keep it

Alert preferences are kept while alerts are on. Listings are kept until shortly after their deadline.

Deleting your information

To delete your information, reply STOP in the InternGH WhatsApp chat and send a message asking for deletion. We'll remove your phone number and alert preferences. To clear your account from a browser, log out of InternGH.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above.
